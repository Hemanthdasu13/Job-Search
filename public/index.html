A pricing recommendation

An analyst asks AI whether to hold price or discount. It finds the competitors' published rate cards, compares them, and says hold. The analysis is clear and consistent throughout.

Three questions:

What did it actually have? Published rate cards. List prices.

What is it assuming? That list price is what competitors charge.

What might be missing? In enterprise deals nobody charges list. The discounting behaviour that decides this question was never in the data.

Nothing in it looked wrong. It was answering a different question.
